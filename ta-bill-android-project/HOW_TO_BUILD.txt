BUILD YOUR APK (free, about 5 minutes, no Android Studio)

1. Go to github.com and sign in (free account).
2. Click "+" > New repository. Name it ta-bill. Keep it Private. Create.
3. Click "uploading an existing file". Unzip this project on your computer and
   drag ALL its contents (the app folder, .github folder, build.gradle,
   settings.gradle, gradle.properties) into the page. Click Commit changes.
   (If the .github folder does not upload: Actions > "set up a workflow
   yourself", paste the text of .github/workflows/build.yml, and commit.)
4. Click the "Actions" tab > "Build APK" > "Run workflow".
5. Wait about 3 to 5 minutes until it shows a green tick.
6. Open the finished run, scroll to "Artifacts", download TA-Bill-APK,
   unzip it and install app-debug.apk on your phone.
If the build shows a red cross, open it and copy the error text to Claude.
